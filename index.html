About the game

Short description

The mansion is mad at the bimbous, and 3,120 specimen jars are scattered around the floor. Carry armfuls, cast spells and file every jar home in this cozy-to-haunted Halloween sorting game, under a never-ending storm.

Long description

Thousands of the bimbous' precious specimen jars are scattered across a spooky mansion: on the floors, down the walls, piled in corners and on the wrong shelves. Bats, brains, frogs, tongues, rubber ducks, the odd homunculus. This Halloween you're their Jarkeeper, and every jar has to find its way home.

Each jar is one of a kind: 26 specimens, 6 colours, 10 catalogue codes, bare or in bloom. The specimen picks the shelf, the colour picks the row, and the code picks the spot. Learn spells from six bimbous to speed things up, and pick how haunted you want the house to be, from a rainy, relaxing Chill mode to a Nightmare of blackouts and earthquakes.

Outside, a never-ending Halloween storm rattles the windows. Inside, the music is cozy and the candles flicker: it plays like watching the rain with a hot chocolate in your hands. Every jar squashes, stretches and clinks into place with sounds made to feel good, and the spells hand you a burst of progress whenever you want one, so there's always a small win around the corner. And then there are the bimbous, six one-of-a-kind housemates you'll fall for the moment you meet them.

Key features

  • 3,120 unique jarsPhysics-driven, each with exactly one place to be.
  • 8 spellsMagnet, Panic, Poltergeist and more, most with upgrades.
  • 3 difficultiesChill, Spooky and Nightmare, with blackouts, quakes and noir episodes.
  • A haunted mansionCandlelit, two floors, under a never-ending storm.
  • Six bimbousCute, powerful housemates who teach you their spells.
  • Built for satisfactionSquash-and-stretch animation and crunchy filing sounds.
